WELCOME TO NSOATRE TECHNICAL INSTITUTE

Skills with
purpose.

Technical education can open doors through practical knowledge, creativity, and the confidence to solve real-world problems.

Nsoatre Technical Institute is a public, mixed technical and vocational institution in Nsoatre, in Ghana’s Bono Region. Its learning focus is practical skills development that can prepare students for employment, further training, and self-employment. Confirm current programme details with the school before applying.
